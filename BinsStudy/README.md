### Why a binning study?
 
Neutrino data are organised into bins of energy and arrival direction (zenith angle only, or zenith and azimuth). They are further separated into event types, which aim to distinguish as well as possible the three flavours and the neutrino/antineutrino distinction. The binning has to be chosen so that every bin is sufficiently populated, which is what `BinsStudy/` investigates.
